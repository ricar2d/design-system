# Primitive Colors

Primitive colors are the raw, named values that form the foundation of the design system. Every semantic token and chart color aliases one of these primitives.

**Source:** `Primitive_colors.json` — Figma variable collection "Primitive Colors"

Each family is numbered from **10 (darkest)** to **95–98 (lightest)**. The **base** step is the truest expression of the hue.
