Primitive colors are the raw, named values that form the foundation of the entire design system. Every semantic token, component color, and chart color is an alias pointing to one of these primitives.

Source: Primitive_colors.json — Figma variable collection "Primitive Colors"

Each family is numbered from 10 (darkest) to 95–98 (lightest). The step marked (base) is the truest expression of that hue.
